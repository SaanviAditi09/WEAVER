# WEAVER website

A minimalist responsive website for WEAVER — free digital & business support for women-led small businesses.

## Email notifications

The form is prepared for Formspree.

1. Create a free Formspree form.
2. Set the destination/notification email to the WEAVER email you want to receive requests at.
3. Copy the Formspree endpoint.
4. In `index.html`, replace:

https://formspree.io/f/YOUR_FORM_ID

with your real endpoint.

The submitted fields will then be emailed to the verified destination.

## Important

Do not put your email password, bank details, API secrets, or other credentials into the website files.

The website intentionally tells applicants not to submit passwords, bank details, payment information, or other highly sensitive information.
