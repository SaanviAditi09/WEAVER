03 / ABOUT

Built for businesses that are building something of their own.

WEAVER is a student-led, free initiative focused on helping women-led small businesses solve practical problems with technology, research and thoughtful systems.

We don't provide investment, tax or legal advice, handle business money, or promise revenue increases. We focus on things we can actually build, analyse and test.

Meet the person
behind WEAVER.

Hi, I'm Saanvi Aditi, the founder of WEAVER.

I'm a student interested in the intersection of business, technology, and problem-solving. I've been building websites and learning software development for several years, and I enjoy using technology to make everyday business tasks simpler and more efficient.

I started WEAVER with a simple idea: small businesses often face problems that don't require expensive professional services — they need practical solutions, technical skills, and someone willing to take the time to build them.

Through WEAVER, I help women-led small businesses with websites, digital systems, business research, data organisation, and practical problem-solving. I work directly with business owners to understand their needs, build or research a solution, and refine it based on what actually works.

WEAVER is completely free. My goal is simple: use my skills to help small businesses spend less time dealing with inefficient systems and more time building their businesses.
