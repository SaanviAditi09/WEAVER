WEAVER / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

WEAVER is a student-led initiative providing free digital and practical business support to women-led small businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and how you can contact us about it.

1. Who operates WEAVER?

WEAVER is currently operated as a student-led initiative by Saanvi Aditi. WEAVER is not currently represented as a registered consulting firm, financial-advisory firm, accounting firm, law firm, or other regulated professional-services organisation. Privacy questions can be sent to weaver.request@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

When you submit a request, we may collect your name, business or brand name, email address, website or public social-media account, business description, requested support, and other information you voluntarily provide in the form or later communications.

3. Why we use your information

We may use it to understand your business, review your request, contact you, ask follow-up questions, build or research a solution, improve WEAVER, maintain reasonable project records, and prevent misuse of the website. We do not sell your personal information or intentionally use request information for unrelated marketing.

4. Information you should not submit

Do not submit passwords, bank credentials, card details, OTPs, authentication codes, government identification numbers, private keys, or other highly sensitive information.

WEAVER does not need access to passwords or banking credentials to provide the services described on this website. Unnecessary sensitive information may be deleted.

5. Form and technology providers

WEAVER may use third-party providers for form processing, hosting, email, storage, analytics, or other technical functions. The request form may use Formspree to receive submissions and forward notifications to the WEAVER email account. Such providers may have their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Email communications

If you submit a request, we may use your email to acknowledge the request, ask questions, discuss the work, deliver information or completed work, and communicate about the project.

7. How long we keep information

WEAVER may retain information as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, complete or follow up on projects, maintain reasonable records, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations. When it is no longer reasonably required, it may be deleted.

8. Security

WEAVER takes reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection and unauthorised access. However, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not submit sensitive credentials through the website.

9. Your choices

You may contact WEAVER to ask about information you submitted, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion where appropriate and legally applicable. We may need to verify the identity of the person making a request.

10. Children

WEAVER's services are intended for business owners and entrepreneurs. The website is not designed to collect personal information from children for business services. If we become aware that information was collected in circumstances where processing was not appropriate, we will take reasonable steps to address it.

11. Third-party links

The website may link to third-party websites or platforms. WEAVER is not responsible for their privacy practices or security. Review their policies before providing personal information.

12. Changes

WEAVER may update this policy when its website, services, technology, or data practices change. The current version will show an updated date.
